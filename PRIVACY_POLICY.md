# Privacy Policy for PassGlobe: Passport Photo ID

**Last updated:** October 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how **PassGlobe: Passport Photo ID** ("we", "our", or "the app") handles your information. 

We believe that your personal photos and signatures are strictly private. Our app is designed to process everything **100% locally on your device** without requiring an account or sending your files to any external server.

---

### 1. No Data Collection or Cloud Uploads
- **No Account Required:** You do not need to register, create an account, or provide personal details (such as email, phone number, or name) to use PassGlobe.
- **On-Device Processing:** All photo cropping, background adjustments, file compression (KB resizing), and signature processing happen directly on your smartphone. We do not operate remote servers to store, process, or view your photos.
- **No Cloud Transmission:** Your photos and signatures never leave your phone.

---

### 2. Device Permissions and How They Are Used
PassGlobe only requests permissions that are strictly necessary for its core features:

- **Camera Permission (`android.permission.CAMERA`):**
  Used only when you choose the "Take Photo" option to capture a new photo for your passport, visa, or ID document. The camera is never accessed in the background.
- **Photos / Storage Permission (`READ_MEDIA_IMAGES` / Storage):**
  Used only when you choose to import an existing photo from your device's photo library, or when you tap "Save to Device Gallery" to save your finished passport photo or print sheet.

You can grant or revoke these permissions at any time through your device settings.

---

### 3. Third-Party Sharing and Tracking
- We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your photos, documents, or personal information with any third parties.
- We do not use third-party analytics trackers, biometric data brokers, or profiling software that monitors your activity across other apps.

---

### 4. Children’s Privacy
PassGlobe does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. Parents and guardians can use the app to create compliant passport or exam photos for their children with complete privacy, as all images stay on the local device.

---

### 5. Data Retention and Deletion
Because we do not collect or store your photos on any server, we retain none of your data. If you delete a photo from your phone's gallery or uninstall the app, all cached working files are removed from your device.

---

If we make any changes to this Privacy Policy, the updated version will be posted here with a revised "Last updated" date.
